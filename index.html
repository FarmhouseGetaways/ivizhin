Who we are

Developers of automation and forward-thinking products.

iVizhin is based in Ramona, CA in the hills above San Diego. We have been building things that mix hardware, software, business and play, since 1996.

Our ag tourism vacation rental properties and a viral farm stand serve as our testing grounds. Everything we make begins as something we needed ourselves. We build it, run it every day on our own operation, and then share it.

That means our products are tested by real guests, real customers and real weather before anyone else sees them. A checkout that has rung up thousands of farm stand sales. A dart scorer that gets played every weekend. A drag strip built for a three-year-old's birthday, that's survived hundreds of guests.

We like cameras, sensors, screens and sound. We pioneered in IoT and evolved to AIoT.

Physical games

Games you can stand in front of.

We build full-size games that put new technology into something you play with your hands. Both of these live in the game barn at Red Barn Ranch and get played every week.

Legend's Stadium Basketball

A two-hoop arcade basketball game built from scratch. We wrote the games, created the graphics and recorded the sounds ourselves. Shots are counted automatically and the scoreboard runs the game like you're in an arcade.

  • Two hoops side by side for head-to-head play with automatic scoring
  • Our own graphics, games and sound design
  • Features a 43" game screen that directs the action
  • Broadcast quality graphics and animation
  • Arcade buttons scroll through the games and start the action
  • Fully customizable with MP3, MP4 and PNG graphics

Legend's Dragway

A four-lane, 1/24-scale drag strip with Sunday racing excitement built in. Two screens show the start, the run and the winner so everyone in the room can watch. It feels like you're racing. It was built for Legend's third birthday and kids LOVE it.

  • Four lanes with broadcast quality graphics and timing displays
  • One screen for racers, one for spectators
  • 1/24 scale, built to be raced hard by small hands

Automation

Smart systems for working farms.

We designed and built the physical systems that run the agricultural side of Red Barn Ranch. Sensors read the conditions on site, a weather station reads Ramona, and the ranch acts on both when nobody is standing there.

Greenhouse

Windows open and close and fans turn on and off based on the weather in Ramona and the readings inside. Temperature and humidity stay where the plants want them.

Chicken coop

Climate

History

Building since 1996.

Early innovation in digital photography and industrial models is central to our beginning. We've been bridging gaps between the physical and digital world for over 30 years.

Modern Arcade Machines

Three standard models and plenty of custom builds. The M1 arcade console rolled up to any television. The M2 table top put a whole arcade on a counter. The M3 was a full-size cabinet with a working coin door, an arcade trackball and a printed control deck under unbreakable lexan.

Our clients ranged from sports and music stars to tech and Fortune 500 companies. The custom artwork, the hand-built decks and the wiring were ours, and that is still how we work.

    The dot-com boom years were spent on digital mapping and building digital presence.
